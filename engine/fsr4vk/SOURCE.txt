fsr4vk v0.4.3 with the AMD fix (GPLv3 provider, MIT AMD FidelityFX data).
This is fsr4vk v0.4.3 (upstream tag v0.4.3, commit 7c04e511195bf4420a060d64df84d625a37457e0) with one patch: it drops the variable descriptor count flag (VK_DESCRIPTOR_BINDING_VARIABLE_DESCRIPTOR_COUNT_BIT) from its three heap bindings, which AMD's Windows driver does not accept. PATCH.md describes it.
